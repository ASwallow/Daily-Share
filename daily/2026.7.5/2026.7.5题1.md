求级数
$$
\huge\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{1}{(4n+1)(4n+2)(4n+3)(4n+4)}
$$
注意到
$$
\huge B(4n+1,4)=\int_0^1x^{4n}(1-x)^3\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{3!}{(4n+1)(4n+2)(4n+3)(4n+4)}
$$
因此
$$
\huge\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{1}{(4n+1)(4n+2)(4n+3)(4n+4)}
$$
$$
\huge =\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{1}{3!}\int_0^1x^{4n}(1-x)^3\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{1}{6}\int_0^1\left(\sum_{n=0}^\infty x^{4n}\right)(1-x)^3\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{1}{6}\int_0^1\frac{(1-x)^3}{1-x^4}\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{1}{6}\int_0^1\frac{2}{1+x}-\frac{1+x}{1+x^2}\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{1}{4}\ln2-\frac{\pi}{24}
$$
