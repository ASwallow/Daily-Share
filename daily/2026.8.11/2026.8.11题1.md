求积分
$$
\huge \int_0^1\ln x\cdot\ln(1-x)\mathrm dx
$$
首先引入两个引理：
>引理一：
$$
\huge\int_0^1x^n\ln x
\mathrm dx$$
$$
\huge =\frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}\ln x-\frac{1}{n+1}\int_0^1x^n\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge=-\frac{1}{(n+1)^2}
$$
引理二：
$$
\huge\zeta(2)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^2}=\frac{\pi^2}{6}
$$

原积分展开得到
$$
\huge -\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{1}{n}\int_0^1x^n\ln x\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{1}{n(n+1)^2}
$$
$$
\huge\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+1}-\frac{1}{(n+1)^2}
$$
$$
\huge =1+1-\zeta(2)=2-\frac{\pi^2}{6}
$$
