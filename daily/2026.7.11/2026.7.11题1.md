求积分
$$
\huge \int_0^\pi e^{\cos x}\cos\sin x\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{1}{2}\int_0^\pi e^{\cos+i\sin x}+e^{\cos-i\sin x}\mathrm dx
$$
由奇偶性得到
$$
\huge =\frac{1}{2}\int_{-\pi}^\pi e^{e^{ix}}\mathrm dx
$$
令$e^{ix}=z$得到
$$
\huge =-\frac{i}{2}\oint_{|z|=1}\frac{e^z}{z}\mathrm dz
$$
$$
\huge =\pi Res[\frac{e^z}{z},0]=\pi
$$
