求积分
$$
\huge\int_0^\infty\ln\left(\frac{1+\theta\cos x}{1-\theta\cos x}\right)\frac{1}{\cos x}\mathrm dx
$$
其中$\theta\in(-1,1)$
令
$$
\huge f(\theta)=\int_0^\infty\ln\left(\frac{1+\theta\cos x}{1-\theta\cos x}\right)\frac{1}{\cos x}\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge \Rightarrow f'(\theta)=\int_0^\infty\frac{\mathrm dx}{1-\theta^2\cos^2x}
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{1+\theta^2}}
$$
从而有
$$
\huge f(
\theta
)=\pi\ln(\sqrt{\theta^2+1}+\theta)
$$
