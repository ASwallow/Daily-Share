求级数
$$
\huge \sum_{i=1}^\infty\sum_{j=1}^\infty\frac{(-1)^{i+j}}{i+j}
$$
考虑到
$$
\huge \frac{(-1)^{i+j}}{i+j}=\int_0^{-1}x^{i+j}\mathrm dx
$$
则有
$$
\huge\int_0^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^\infty\sum_{j=1}^\infty x^{i+j}\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\int_0^{-1}\frac{x}{(1-x)^2}\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\ln 2-\frac{1}{2}
$$
