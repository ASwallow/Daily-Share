求积分
$$
\huge \int_0^1 x\bmod x^2\mathrm dx
$$
由恒等式
$$
\huge x\bmod y=x-y\left\lfloor\frac{x}{y}\right\rfloor
$$
那么
$$
\huge \int_0^1 x\bmod x^2\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\int_0^1 x-x^2\left\lfloor\frac{1}{x}\right\rfloor\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge=-\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\int_{\frac{1}{n}}^{\frac{1}{n+1}}x-x^2\left\lfloor\frac{1}{x}\right\rfloor\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =-\sum_{n=1}^\infty\int_{\frac{1}{n}}^{\frac{1}{n+1}}x-nx^2\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{1}{n^2}-\frac{1}{(n+1)^2}\right]-\frac{n}{3}\left[\frac{1}{n^3}-\frac{1}{(n+1)^3}\right]
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{3}\sum_{n=1}^\infty n\left(\frac{1}{n^3}-\frac{1}{(n+1)^3}\right)
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{3}\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{1}{n^3}
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{3}\zeta(3)
$$
