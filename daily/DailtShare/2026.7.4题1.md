求级数
$$
\huge \sum_{n=1}^\infty x^n\sum_{k=1}^n\frac{1}{k}
$$
的收敛域和和函数
首先看收敛域
$$
\huge \lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\left|\dfrac{\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n+1}}{\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}}\right|=1
$$
在$x=\pm1$时，通项不收敛，因此显然原级数不收敛，收敛域为$(-1,1)$
再来看和函数
令
$$
\huge\begin{matrix}
f(x)&=&x&+&(1+\frac{1}{2})x^2&+&(1+\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3})x^3&+&\cdots\\
xf(x)&=&&&x^2&+&(1+\frac{1}{2})x^3&+&\cdots\\
(1-x)f(x)&=&x&+&\frac{1}{2}x^2&+&\frac{1}{3}x^3&+&\cdots
\end{matrix}
$$
$$
\huge(1-x)f(x)=\ln(1-x)
$$
$$
\huge\huge \sum_{n=1}^\infty x^n\sum_{k=1}^n\frac{1}{k}=\frac{\ln(1-x)}{1-x}
$$
