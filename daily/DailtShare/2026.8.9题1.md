求级数
$$
\huge \sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{1}{n^2\binom{2n}{n}}
$$
首先由于
$$
\huge \frac{n}{2}B(n,n)=\frac{1}{\binom{2n}{n}}
$$
从而
$$
\huge =\int_0^1\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{2n}(t-t^2)^{n-1}\mathrm dt
$$
$$
\huge =-\int_0^1\frac{\ln(1-t+t^2)}{2t(1-t)}\mathrm dt
$$
$$
\huge =-\frac{1}{2}\int_0^1\frac{\ln(1-t+t^2)}{t}+\frac{\ln(1-t+t^2)}{1-t}\mathrm dt
$$
不难看出
$$
\huge \int_0^1\frac{\ln(1-t+t^2)}{t}\mathrm dt=\int_0^1\frac{\ln(1-t+t^2)}{1-t}\mathrm dt
$$
从而
$$
\huge = -\int_0^1\frac{\ln(1-t+t^2)}{t}\mathrm dt
$$
$$
\huge =\int_0^1\frac{\ln(1+t)}{t}-\frac{\ln(1+t^3)}{t}\mathrm dt
$$
不难看出答案为
$$
\huge = \frac{\pi^2}{12}\left(1-\frac{1}{3}\right)=\frac{\pi^2}{18}
$$
