求极限
$$
\huge\lim_{x\rightarrow0}\frac{\tan\tan x-\sin\sin x}{\tan x-\sin x}
$$
首先由泰勒展开式得到
$$
\huge\tan x-\sin x\sim\frac{1}{2}x^3
$$
$$
\huge\tan\tan x\sim\tan x+\frac{1}{3}\tan^3x+\frac{1}{3}\left(\tan x+\frac{1}{3}\tan^3x\right)^3
$$
$$
\huge\sim\tan x+\frac{2}{3}\tan^3x
$$
$$
\huge\sin\sin x\sim\sin x-\frac{1}{6}\sin^3x-\frac{1}{6}\left(\sin x-\frac{1}{6}\sin^3x\right)^3
$$
$$
\huge\sim\sin x-\frac{1}{3}\sin^3x
$$
那么
$$
\huge\tan\tan x-\sin\sin x\sim\tan x+\frac{2}{3}\tan^3x-\sin x+\frac{1}{3}\sin^3x
$$
$$
\huge\sim\frac{1}{2}x^3+x^3=\frac{3}{2}x^3
$$
因此
$$
\huge\lim_{x\rightarrow0}\frac{\tan\tan x-\sin\sin x}{\tan x-\sin x}=3
$$
