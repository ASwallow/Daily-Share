已知函数$$f(x)=|x|$$的傅里叶级数前$n$项为$S_n(x)$
求
$$
\huge T_n = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\left[f(x)-S_n(x)\right]^2\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}T_n
$$
先求出目标$Fourier$级数
$$
\huge S_n(x)=\frac{\pi}{2}+\sum_{k=1}^n\frac{2}{\pi}\frac{-1+(-1)^k}{k^2}\cos(kx)
$$
$$
\huge S_n(x)=\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{4}{\pi}\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\rfloor}\frac{1}{(2k+1)^2}\cos(2k+1)x
$$
则要求的数列为
$$
\huge \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{16}{\pi^2}\left[\sum_{k=\lfloor\frac{n+1}{2}\rfloor}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(2k+1)^2}\cos(2k+1)x\right]^2\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{8}{\pi^3}\sum_{k=\lfloor\frac{n+1}{2}\rfloor}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(2k+1)^2}\int_0^{2\pi}\cos^2(2k+1)x\mathrm dx
$$
$$
\huge = \frac{8}{\pi^2}\left[\beta(4)-\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\rfloor}\frac{1}{(2k+1)^2}\right]
$$
$$
\huge\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\huge = \frac{8}{\pi^2}\left[\beta(4)-\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\rfloor}\frac{1}{(2k+1)^2}\right]=0
$$
