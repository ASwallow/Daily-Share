求积分
$$
\huge \int_0^1\frac{\ln(1+t^\alpha)}{t}\mathrm dt
$$
将被积函数展开得到
$$
\huge \int_0^1\frac{1}{t}\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n}t^{\alpha n}\mathrm dt
$$
$$
\huge =\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n}\int_0^1t^{\alpha n}\mathrm dt
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{1}{\alpha}\sum_{n+1}^\infty\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n^2}
$$
$$
\huge =\frac{1}{\alpha}\left[\zeta(2)-\frac{1}{4}\zeta(2)\right]=\frac{1}{\alpha}\beta(2)
$$
$$
\huge = \frac{\pi^2}{12 \alpha}
$$
